Data Science Job Market Trends and Salary Analysis in 2024¶

Project Overview¶

Organizations increasingly rely on data science professionals to support data-driven decision-making, business intelligence, predictive analytics, and artificial intelligence initiatives. Understanding salary trends, hiring demand, and workforce characteristics within the data science industry can help organizations make informed hiring decisions and assist professionals in planning their careers.

The purpose of this project is to analyze and integrate two publicly available datasets related to data science salaries and job postings. Through exploratory data analysis, data preprocessing, data integration, and visualization techniques, the project identifies patterns and trends within the data science labor market.

The analysis focuses on:

  • Salary differences across data science job roles
  • The impact of experience level on compensation
  • Hiring demand for data science positions
  • Remote work trends within the industry
  • The relationship between job demand and salary levels

The findings generated from this analysis provide valuable insights for both hiring organizations and data science professionals seeking career opportunities.

Data Sources¶

Dataset 1: Data Science Salaries 2024 Dataset¶

Source: Kaggle

Description: This dataset contains salary information for data science professionals, including job title, experience level, company size, remote work ratio, and salary in USD.

Dataset 2: Data Science Canada March 2024 Jobs Dataset¶

Source: Kaggle

Description: This dataset contains data science job posting information, including job title, company information, experience level, contract type, location, and employment sector.

These datasets were selected because they provide complementary information regarding compensation trends, workforce demand, experience requirements, and employment characteristics within the data science industry.

Project Objectives¶

The objectives of this project are to:

  1. Perform exploratory data analysis to identify key data points.
  2. Evaluate the quality of the selected datasets.
  3. Apply data preprocessing techniques to improve data quality.
  4. Integrate multiple datasets into a unified collection.
  5. Create visualizations that reveal important trends and patterns.
  6. Analyze relationships among multiple data attributes.
  7. Derive actionable insights from the visualizations.
  8. Communicate findings through a data-driven narrative.

Data Quality Evaluation¶

  • Completeness
  • Consistency
  • Accuracy
  • Duplicate records
  • Missing values

The datasets were examined using summary statistics, data type inspection, missing value analysis, and duplicate record detection.

The evaluation indicated that both datasets contain sufficient information for analyzing salary trends and job market demand within the data science industry. Any identified missing values and duplicate records were addressed during the preprocessing stage to improve data quality and reliability.

  • Removal of duplicate records

Data Blending¶

The two datasets were integrated to create a single analytical dataset.

Job titles from both datasets were standardized and used to create a common job category field.

The salary dataset contributes compensation information while the jobs dataset contributes hiring demand information.

Combining these datasets enables more comprehensive analysis and satisfies the project requirement for integrating multiple data sources.

In [9]:
salary_clean["job_category"] = salary_clean["job_title"]
jobs_clean["job_category"] = jobs_clean["title"]

salary_summary = (
    salary_clean
    .groupby("job_category")["salary_in_usd"]
    .mean()
    .reset_index()
)

salary_summary.rename(
    columns={"salary_in_usd":"avg_salary"},
    inplace=True
)

job_summary = (
    jobs_clean
    .groupby("job_category")
    .size()
    .reset_index(name="job_count")
)

merged_df = pd.merge(
    salary_summary,
    job_summary,
    on="job_category",
    how="inner"

Insights and Findings¶

The visualizations reveal several important trends within the data science job market.

Key Findings¶

  1. Senior and executive-level professionals earn significantly higher salaries than entry-level employees.

  2. Certain specialized positions, including Data Scientist, Machine Learning Engineer, and Data Engineer, command the highest compensation levels.

  3. Remote work remains a significant component of the data science workforce.

  4. Larger organizations generally provide higher average salaries than smaller organizations.

  5. Strong demand exists for positions that also offer competitive compensation.

  6. Salary trends and hiring demand are closely related across many job categories.

Conclusion¶

This analysis successfully combined multiple datasets to examine salary trends, job demand, experience requirements, and workplace characteristics within the data science industry. The findings provide valuable information for employers, recruiters, and job seekers making data-driven decisions.
